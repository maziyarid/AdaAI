## What was learned (not a dump)

High-confidence current operating knowledge:

- Quality is information gain and sitewide consistency, not word count.
- Intent collision is an IA problem; Google has not published a “cannibalization ranking system”.
- Refresh substance; date-only jobs are banned.
- Scaled unreviewed pages and combinatory template URLs are site-level trust failures (Google spam policy + Mueller 2026).
- Expired-domain ranking plays are official spam (supersedes 2020 buy-expired advice).
- Keep redirects ≥1 year; 301 is not a full-equity pipe.
- Schema / E-E-A-T / sitemap are not indexation tickets (Illyes 2025 list).
- llms.txt is not a ranking project.
- Service URLs own commercial intent; the blog does not.

Do-not-adopt (claim-level): fake reportage clicks, downtime recrawl, DA inflators, Money Robot, popups, friends-search, most-visited auto-refresh, fake article stars, detector hacks, medical content in this factory, negative SEO.
