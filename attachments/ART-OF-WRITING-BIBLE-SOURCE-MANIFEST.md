## Reconciliation policy

A rule entered the canonical Bible when it was repeated across independent corpus slices, supported by both formal and community evidence, strongly supported inside one clearly scoped register, or corroborated by current external research.

Conflicting findings were converted into register-dependent rules rather than flattened into false universal rules.

Key examples: passive vs active voice; long vs short sentences; question headings; colloquial forms; ZWNJ; English terminology.

## Explicit exclusions

The Bible does not clone PhilosophyCafe or any living writer; treat reposts/quotations as authorial-style evidence; treat service claims as facts; treat natural wording as factual validation; target AI-detector evasion; or make Teznevise-specific orthography a universal Persian rule.
