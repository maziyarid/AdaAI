# Slice D — Persian Human Editorial Writing: Public Telegram Communities

> Evidence pack for the shared `thesis-persian-human-editorial-writing` skill.
> Slice scope: public Telegram channels & discussion groups on research
> methodology, statistics, thesis / academic writing, and university topics
> (Iranian Persian).
>
> Fetch method: HTML preview via `https://t.me/s/<channel>` — only
> publicly-indexable posts. No private groups, no closed channels.
> Date window: mostly 2023 – 2026 posts (a few older evergreen posts noted).
>
> Orthography convention for this file: **no U+200C (ZWNJ) anywhere.**
> Where standard orthography uses a half space, this file uses either the
> concatenated form (`میخواهیم`, `پایاننامه`) or a plain ASCII space
> (`می خواهیم`, `پایان نامه`). Constructed examples in §10 strictly follow
> the same rule.

## 1. Research Slice

- **Slice ID:** D
- **Medium:** Public Telegram channels (`t.me/s/<name>` HTML preview) —
  broadcast channels + a handful of visible discussion / questionnaire
  threads.
- **Register mix observed:**
  - `PROFESSIONAL_EXPLAINER` — methodology / statistics teachers walking
    through a concept in short chunks (dominant on `Amar_phd`,
    `qualitativestudies`, `SPSS_SEM`, `researcheditor_ir`).
  - `PROMOTIONAL_BUREAUCRATIC` — thesis / editing / SPSS-analysis services
    advertising course packages, deadlines, prices (dominant on
    `payannameh_andishe_no`, `analysisacademy`, `dr_sharifian_isi`,
    `PLSClub`, `spss23`).
  - `ANNOUNCEMENT_BUREAUCRATIC` — university-affiliated channels with
    exam-registration / deadline notices (`phdex`, `kurdistanuni`,
    `Arshad_Phd_Tj`, `finance_thesis`).
  - `CONVERSATIONAL_STUDENT` — questionnaire-recruitment posts from thesis
    students, visible in `habibi_res_sta` and forwarded into general
    research channels; still Telegram-broadcast prose (no visible replies),
    but written in second-person and casual register.
- **Why this slice matters for the skill:** Telegram is the largest Persian
  informal channel for academic explainers. Its voice sits between the
  bureaucratic-formal register of Iranian journals and the casual register
  of student-oriented Instagram/Telegram promotion. Extracting the
  explanatory voice while stripping the CTA / emoji clutter is the exact
  transformation the parent skill needs to catch.

## 3. Findings (LANGUAGE / FACT / SEO tags separated)

Each finding is tagged:

- `[LANG]` — language / register / style observation the parent skill can use.
- `[FACT]` — a subject-matter claim; may be right, wrong, or contested;
  the skill must **not** endorse it.
- `[SEO]` — thesis-service / promotional-intent signal (useful only for the
  anti-patterns / promotional-tone section of the skill).

### 3.1 Voice, register, tone

- **F-01 `[LANG]`** — Teacher-explainer posts open with a bare bolded term
  or a definition, not a hook. Typical shape: `🔹 پایایی چیست؟` then a
  2–4-line definition. Example S-01, S-07, S-15, S-33 all use this
  opener.
- **F-02 `[LANG]`** — Very short paragraphs. A single sentence per line is
  the norm; the "paragraph" is the emoji-bullet plus one clause. Even
  long posts on S-07 (SEM) break every clause onto its own line.
- **F-03 `[LANG]`** — Second-person plural (`میدونید که...`, `یادتون باشه`)
  dominates coaching / thesis-writing channels (S-05, S-13, S-23). It
  co-occurs with colloquial verb endings (`می ره`, `میشه`, `می تونه`).
- **F-04 `[LANG]`** — Third-person impersonal (`می شود`, `می توان`,
  `پژوهشگر باید`) dominates the reference-style explainer channels (S-07,
  S-28, S-33, S-18). Same author will code-switch to `-ون` colloquial in
  a promo paragraph inside the same post.
- **F-05 `[LANG]`** — Salutations and religious formulas frame roughly a
  quarter of S-07's posts (`اَلسَّلامُ عَلَیْکَ یا اَبا عَبْدِ اللهِ`,
  Fatimeh birthday greetings). This is diagnostic of Iranian bureaucratic
  Persian; the parent skill's editorial voice should treat these as
  channel-local decoration and strip them.
- **F-06 `[LANG]`** — First-person plural authorial "we" (`همانطور که
  دیدیم`, `در این پست بررسی میکنیم`) shows up in the more polished
  masterclass promos (S-08, S-19, S-14). It reads more marketing than
  scholarly.
- **F-07 `[LANG]`** — Rhetorical yes/no questions as section headers
  (`چرا دو پژوهشگر روی یک موضوع یکسان جستجو میکنند اما یکی فقط ۳۰۰ مقاله
  پیدا میکند و دیگری بیش از ۳۰۰۰ مقاله؟!`) are the single most common
  hook in analysis-service promo (S-08, S-19, S-29). They sit above the
  fold and are almost always followed by `پاسخ فقط یک کلمه است: مهارت`.
- **F-08 `[LANG]`** — Reader-flattery openers (`دوست عزیز`, `همراهان
  گرامی`, `پژوهشگران عزیز`) mark the transition from information to CTA.
  When the parent skill de-editorializes Telegram prose, this vocative is
  the reliable seam.

### 3.2 Formatting norms

- **F-09 `[LANG]`** — Emoji bullets replace list markers: `🔹`, `✅`, `▪️`,
  `◾️`, `🔸`, `📌`, `🔺`, `🔴`, `🟢`, `1️⃣ 2️⃣ 3️⃣`. Across S-01/03/06/07/08/
  15/16/18/19/29/32, no post used `-` or `*` as an ASCII bullet.
- **F-10 `[LANG]`** — Numbered enumerations always use the boxed digit
  emoji (`1️⃣ … 5️⃣`) or Persian numerals (`۱ … ۵`), never Arabic-Indic
  digits alone.
- **F-11 `[LANG]`** — Bold in Telegram is achieved by wrapping in `**...**`
  or by rendering the entire mini-paragraph in the post title emoji frame
  (`🔺 عنوان: ...`). Editors converting such posts to publishable web copy
  must promote the emoji-titled block to a real `<h3>` / `**bold**` and
  drop the emoji.
- **F-12 `[LANG]`** — Hashtag clusters trail almost every teacher-post
  (`#مقاله_نویسی #پایان_نامه #ISI #دکتری #ارشد #روش_تحقیق`; S-04, S-14,
  S-18). Underscores replace spaces because Telegram breaks hashtags on
  whitespace. Editorial rewrites should drop the whole cluster.
- **F-13 `[LANG]`** — CTA blocks are visually separated by rulers of
  repeated symbols (`➖➖➖➖➖➖➖`, `〰〰〰〰〰`, `_ _ _ _ _`; S-07, S-12,
  S-17). This is the second reliable seam for de-editorialization.
- **F-14 `[LANG]`** — Contact-info tail is nearly universal: WhatsApp
  number, Telegram `@handle`, sometimes an Instagram link — always
  formatted `📞09xxxxxxxxx` or `تماس: 09xxxxxxxxx`. Present in S-08, S-09,
  S-12, S-14, S-17, S-19, S-20, S-22, S-35.
- **F-15 `[LANG]`** — In-post inline URLs are rare; long URLs are
  wrapped as button-style links via markdown text with a big emoji
  arrow (`🌐 analysisacademy.com`, `👇 [Link]`).

### 3.3 Methodology / statistics teacher voice

- **F-16 `[LANG]`** — Concept-then-definition-then-example is the default
  micro-structure (S-01, S-07, S-15). Rare use of "why the student
  should care" framing outside promo posts.
- **F-17 `[LANG]`** — The teacher voice keeps English technical terms in
  parentheses next to the Persian: `همبستگی کانونی (Canonical Correlation
  Analysis — CCA)`, `آمار پارامتریک (Parametric)`, `آزادتوزیع
  (distribution-free)`. Consistent across S-01, S-07, S-08, S-33.
- **F-18 `[LANG]`** — Software names are transliterated *and* Latin-cased
  in the same post: `لیزرل / LISREL`, `اموس / AMOS`, `اسمارت پی ال اس /
  SmartPLS`, `ایویوز / EViews`. S-07, S-08, S-09, S-20.
- **F-19 `[LANG]`** — `آزمون` is the standard word for a statistical test;
  `تست` is only used inside a compound English term (`تی تست`, `Post-hoc
  test`) and never bare.
- **F-20 `[LANG]`** — Emphasis quotes: teachers use gershayn `«...»`, not
  curly quotes. Present in S-07, S-21, S-23, S-33.
- **F-21 `[FACT]`** — S-10's PLS fit-index table (`R² 0.19/0.33/0.67`;
  `f² 0.02/0.15/0.35`; `Q² 0.02/0.15/0.35`; `GOF 0.01/0.25/0.36`) is
  **partly wrong**: the widely-cited GOF thresholds are 0.01 / 0.25 /
  0.36 (Wetzels et al. 2009) which the post got right, but conflating Q²
  cutoffs with f² cutoffs is a common error and appears in several
  channels. Do not endorse.
- **F-22 `[FACT]`** — S-11's ranking "Iran fourth-highest average IQ 2025
  per International IQ Test" is **not reliable**: `internationaliqtest.com`
  is a self-selected online quiz, not a research instrument. Do not
  endorse.
- **F-23 `[FACT]`** — Multiple channels (S-01, S-07, S-33) claim a
  regression-based parametric assumption must include normality of the
  raw variables. This is **an overreach**: OLS normality assumption is on
  the *residuals*, not the raw variables. Do not endorse.

### 3.4 Promotional / bureaucratic language (thesis-service channels)

- **F-24 `[SEO]`** — Boilerplate CTA phrasing: `جهت ثبت سفارش با ادمین
  در ارتباط باشید`, `نگارش تضمینی پروپوزال`, `پذیرش و چاپ تضمینی مقاله
  ISI`, `تحویل فوری با تضمین کیفیت`. Present verbatim across S-17, S-27,
  S-35, S-14.
- **F-25 `[SEO]`** — "Guarantee" language is a stock phrase, not a claim:
  `تضمینی` co-occurs with `نگارش`, `چاپ`, `پذیرش`, `اکسپت`. When the
  parent skill's anti-patterns section lists Persian marketing tics,
  `تضمینی` is a top item.
- **F-26 `[SEO]`** — Price and course-length lists follow a canonical
  emoji frame: `📌 مدت زمان دوره:`, `📌 هزینه:`, `📌 نحوه برگزاری:
  آنلاین`, `📌 نحوه ثبت نام:`. S-14, S-19, S-30, S-08.
- **F-27 `[SEO]`** — Scarcity phrasing: `تنها ۵ ظرفیت نهایی باقی مانده
  است`, `اولویت با تکمیل پیش پرداخت`, `دوره در آستانه تکمیل ظرفیت`. S-08,
  S-19, S-14.
- **F-28 `[SEO]`** — Discount codes and student pricing: `تخفیف
  دانشجویی`, `کد تخفیف` — S-03 (Lingano ad), S-14, S-19. The word
  `فرصت محدود` shows up but is rarer than expected; scarcity here is
  timed by course start-date instead.
- **F-29 `[LANG]`** — Bureaucratic university announcements (S-24, S-25,
  S-26, S-35) use nominal-heavy, agent-hiding grammar: `تمدید مهلت دفاع
  از رساله دکتری تا ۳۰ مهر`, `به اطلاع می رساند`, `شایان توجه است`,
  `الزامی است`. These templates are strong AI-translationese lookalikes
  when translated from English (`is required`, `it should be noted`).
- **F-30 `[LANG]`** — Bureaucratic register uses the passive impersonal
  (`می شود`, `اعلام می گردد`, `منتشر گردید`) and archaic verb inflections
  (`می باشد`) more often than the natural indicative (`است`, `منتشر شد`).
  The parent skill should treat both `می باشد` and `اعلام می گردد` as
  bureaucratic markers and rewrite to `است` / `اعلام شد` in editorial
  prose.

### 3.5 Fingilish / transliteration / code-mixing

- **F-31 `[LANG]`** — Software names alternate Perso-Arabic and Latin
  script in the same paragraph: `لیزرل، اموس` and `LISREL, AMOS`. Some
  channels prefer the Latin form (S-08 for `Bibliometric`, `Mplus`,
  `CiteSpace`), others prefer the Persian transliteration (S-07 favors
  `اموس`). Editorial policy needed.
- **F-32 `[LANG]`** — Casual "Fingilish" mixing appears in coaching /
  motivational posts: `submit`, `ریجکشن`, `پارافریز`, `اکسپت`,
  `سابمیت`, `منیو اسکریپت` (= manuscript), `آندر ریویو`. S-14, S-15,
  S-19, S-33. Standard editorial Persian would prefer `ارسال`, `رد`,
  `بازنویسی`, `پذیرش`, `دست نویس`, `در دست داوری`.
- **F-33 `[LANG]`** — `چت جی پی تی` / `ChatGPT` / `GPT` used in the same
  post (S-14, S-19, S-29). No consistent orthography.
- **F-34 `[LANG]`** — Latin acronyms are kept unchanged: `ISI`, `Q1`,
  `Scopus`, `DOI`, `IQ`, `SEM`, `AVE`, `HTMT`, `PLS`, `GOF`, `ANOVA`,
  `ANCOVA`, `GEE`. No Persianization observed.
- **F-35 `[LANG]`** — Publisher / vendor names stay Latin: `Web of
  Science`, `Elsevier`, `Turnitin`, `Paperpile`, `Zotero`, `Endnote`,
  `Elicit`, `Consensus`, `ResearchRabbit`. Persianized forms
  (`ترنیتین`, `پیپرپایل`) do NOT appear.

### 3.6 Question / heading patterns

- **F-36 `[LANG]`** — The bolded "چیست؟" question is the workhorse
  section header: `افیلیشن چیست؟`, `سایتیشن چیست؟`, `چکیده چیست؟`. S-15,
  S-01, S-28.
- **F-37 `[LANG]`** — Sub-headings routinely start with a numbered
  question mark: `❓سوال اول: آیا میتونیم از این قسمت صرفنظر کنیم؟` and
  the answer is boxed as `✅ پاسخ:`. S-15, S-33.
- **F-38 `[LANG]`** — "N nokte" (`N نکته`) is the most common list-post
  headline template: `۵ نکته کلیدی در مقدمه نویسی`, `۱۰ نکته در باب
  ویراستاری`, `۳ کار عملی که این هفته انجام دهید`. S-04, S-06, S-12.
- **F-39 `[LANG]`** — The generic hero line `تفاوت X و Y چیست؟` is the
  most viral explainer template. Present in S-01 (Pearson vs Canonical),
  S-07 (parametric vs non-parametric), S-09 (PLS vs AMOS), S-10 (PLS vs
  LISREL).

### 3.7 Rhythm, syntax, punctuation

- **F-40 `[LANG]`** — Sentences average 12 – 18 words in explainer prose,
  much shorter than a typical Persian print-media sentence. Long
  bureaucratic sentences (30+ words) appear in announcement channels
  (S-24, S-25).
- **F-41 `[LANG]`** — Colon-then-list is the dominant syntactic form:
  `شرایط شرکت: ...`, `مزایای دوره: ...`, `سرفصل ها: ...`.
- **F-42 `[LANG]`** — Ellipsis (`...`) is used to imply "and more" at the
  end of feature lists, especially in course promos (`چگونگی محاسبه پایایی
  ترکیبی، بارهای عاملی، شاخص های برازش و ...`). Editorial rewrites should
  remove or replace with a concrete last item.
- **F-43 `[LANG]`** — `که` chains ("comma-that-comma-that") are common:
  `پژوهشگری که در جهان امروز میخواهد مقاله بنویسد و به دنبال چاپ در ژورنالی
  است که ایمپکت بالا داشته باشد باید...`. Editorial pass: break into two
  sentences.
- **F-44 `[LANG]`** — Nominal-heavy constructions (`مورد بررسی قرار
  گرفت`, `مورد استفاده قرار میگیرد`, `به عمل آمد`) are stock in
  university announcements (S-24, S-25) but rare in the teacher-voice
  channels. This is the exact bureaucratic tic the parent skill should
  compress to a plain verb (`بررسی شد`, `استفاده میشود`, `انجام شد`).
- **F-45 `[LANG]`** — Two-word conjunctive adverbs: `به عبارت دیگر`,
  `به طور کلی`, `از سوی دیگر`, `همچنین`, `بنابراین`. Overused across S-01,
  S-07, S-18, S-28. AI-generated Persian often over-clusters these.
- **F-46 `[LANG]`** — Dashes are rendered as em-dash or `–` inside stat
  ranges (`0.02 – 0.35`) and as `-` between Persian clauses. Curly quotes
  and true typographic em-dashes are absent — everything is ASCII.
- **F-47 `[LANG]`** — Number-and-percent formatting: `۳۰ درصد` beats
  `۳۰٪` in prose channels; the `%` symbol appears mostly in stat tables.

### 3.8 Meta / conversational

- **F-48 `[LANG]`** — Student questionnaire posts (S-34) adopt a
  formulaic opener: `سلام وقت بخیر 🌸 / برای تکمیل پژوهش پایان نامه خودم
  به مشارکت شما نیاز دارم`. Ending: `پیشاپیش سپاسگزارم 🙏`. Two visible
  student posts on the same channel used near-identical templates,
  suggesting a shared "how-to" instruction upstream.
- **F-49 `[LANG]`** — Motivational posts (S-01, S-19, S-23) borrow
  self-help register (`باور کن که میتونی`, `صبور باش`, `یادت باشه`)
  and are often forwarded from external life-coaching channels; they
  contaminate the academic register.
- **F-50 `[LANG]`** — When a channel forwards from another (visible
  `Forwarded from …` header), the forward keeps its original register.
  A rigorous editorial pass has to detect the boundary; simple
  concatenation of a channel's posts will mix five registers per week.
- **F-51 `[SEO]`** — Thesis-service channels frequently list *every
  discipline they cover* as a bullet-mass (S-17 posts a hundred+
  engineering sub-fields in one message). This is SEO for Telegram
  search, not editorial content.

## 6. Syntax & Rhythm Observations

- **S-A** — In explainer prose, average sentence length ≈ 14 words.
  Bureaucratic prose ≈ 28 words (long single-sentence announcements).
  AI-translated Persian tends to sit around 25 – 30 words per sentence
  and is a giveaway.
- **S-B** — Persian preposition + infinitive is very common: `برای دستیابی
  به X`, `به منظور بررسی X`, `به دلیل وجود X`. Two consecutive
  `به X + noun` phrases in one clause read as translated English.
  Editorial rewrite: convert one to a verb.
- **S-C** — Ezafe over-chaining (`تحلیل داده های کمی پژوهش های مدیریت
  عملکرد سازمانی`) is common in academic Telegram. Five-noun ezafe chains
  are frequent; six+ read as awkward. Editorial pass: break with a
  preposition or a relative clause.
- **S-D** — `که ... است` (`that ... is`) copular tails are the single
  most identifiable translationese pattern. Real Persian teacher voice
  drops the copula: `پایایی سازگاری درونی گویه ها` beats `پایایی، سازگاری
  درونی گویه ها است`.
- **S-E** — Enumeration parallelism is fragile: teacher posts routinely
  mix noun-phrase items with verb-phrase items in the same list
  (`سرفصل ها: تحلیل عاملی، رگرسیون خطی، آموزش نحوه رسم نمودار`). A
  clean editorial version would pick one shape.
- **S-F** — Coordinating with `و` between more than three items is
  rare; over three items always shift to comma-and-final-`و`.
- **S-G** — Rhetorical negation (`نه اینکه X، بلکه Y`) shows up in
  senior-voice explainer (S-04, S-08, S-13). This IS natural Persian;
  keep it. AI translation of `not X but Y` reproduces this well, so it
  is not a diagnostic — the diagnostic is when it disappears entirely
  from a "senior-voice" AI text.

## 8. Anti-AI-Translationese Notes (Persian-specific)

Patterns that scream "translated by a model" and how a fluent Persian
editor tightens them:

1. **`می باشد` epidemic.** Any sentence ending in `می باشد` reads as
   translated. Replace with `است` unless the copula is genuinely aspectual
   (rare).
2. **Nominal chains.** `مورد بررسی قرار گرفت / مورد استفاده قرار میگیرد /
   مورد ارزیابی قرار خواهد گرفت` → collapse to `بررسی شد / استفاده میشود
   / ارزیابی خواهد شد`.
3. **Passive with `توسط`.** `این مقاله توسط دکتر X نوشته شد` reads
   translated. Prefer `دکتر X این مقاله را نوشت` or `این مقاله را دکتر X
   نوشت`.
4. **`همانطور که میدانیم / همانطور که مشاهده کردیم / همانطور که
   میبینیم`.** Over-used connective; delete in most cases.
5. **`این نکته بسیار مهم است که ...`** — direct calque of "it is
   important to note that". Prefer a bare declarative.
6. **`لازم به ذکر است ...`** — same shape, same problem.
7. **`در نظر گرفتن این نکته ضروری است ...`** — model-generated hedge; the
   fact itself is the point.
8. **`با توجه به آنچه گفته شد ...`** — filler; delete or rewrite as
   `بنابراین`.
9. **Definite article carry-over.** English "the" often surfaces as
   `این` when a bare noun would do. `این پژوهشگر باید ...` (calqued from
   "The researcher must ...") — drop `این` unless a specific instance is
   already in scope.
10. **`رعایت X` overuse.** `رعایت اصول` / `رعایت نکات` — the abstract
    noun+ رعایت construction stacks up in AI output; the verb version
    (`اصول را رعایت کنید`) reads more human.
11. **Fake elegance.** `دستیابی به بینشی عمیق تر نسبت به`, `درک عمیق تری
    از`, `مسیر تحول آفرین` — translated marketing polish that a human
    editor would replace with a concrete claim.
12. **Symmetric-list translationese.** English "not only … but also …"
    → Persian AI produces `نه تنها ... بلکه همچنین ...`. Native Persian
    is happy with a bare `و` most of the time.
13. **Numbered-list bloat.** AI Persian loves seven-item lists with a
    one-line item each. Real Persian teacher voice caps around 4–5.
14. **`جالب است بدانید که ...` filler.** Delete unless the "did-you-know"
    frame is genuinely load-bearing.

## 9. Constructed Before/After Examples (20+)

All examples below use NO U+200C ZWNJ. `میخواهیم` is the concatenated form;
`می خواهیم` is with ASCII space. Neither uses the half-space character.

### 9.1 Overly-promotional thesis-service prose → clean explainer prose

**EX-01 — `تضمینی` boilerplate**

- UNNATURAL_OR_WEAK: `پذیرش و چاپ تضمینی مقاله ISI با تضمین ۱۰۰ درصدی
  کیفیت و تحویل فوری در سریعترین زمان ممکن.`
- BETTER_PERSIAN: `برای انتشار مقاله ISI، ابتدا کیفیت پژوهش را با یک
  داور همتراز بسنجید و سپس ژورنالی متناسب با دامنه موضوع انتخاب کنید.`
- WHY: The original stacks three unfalsifiable marketing claims
  (`تضمینی`, `۱۰۰ درصد`, `تحویل فوری`). The revision replaces the pitch
  with an actionable step, which is what an editorial reader wants.

**EX-02 — Hero-question payoff**

- UNNATURAL_OR_WEAK: `چرا برخی مقالهها پذیرفته میشوند و برخی رد؟ پاسخ
  فقط یک کلمه است: مهارت!`
- BETTER_PERSIAN: `پذیرش یا رد یک مقاله بیش از هر چیز به کیفیت طرح
  مسئله و استحکام روش شناسی بستگی دارد.`
- WHY: The one-word answer trope is course-sales rhetoric. Editorial
  prose names the two mechanisms directly.

**EX-03 — Scarcity + emoji**

- UNNATURAL_OR_WEAK: `⚠️ تنها ۵ ظرفیت نهایی باقی مانده است و اولویت با
  تکمیل پیش پرداخت خواهد بود. 🔥 همین امروز ثبت نام کن!`
- BETTER_PERSIAN: `ثبت نام تا سه شنبه ۳ شهریور باز است؛ ظرفیت محدود
  اعلام شده است.`
- WHY: Editorial copy states the deadline as a neutral fact and drops
  the countdown emoji + imperative.

**EX-04 — Guarantee-stack**

- UNNATURAL_OR_WEAK: `نگارش تضمینی پروپوزال دقیق برای موضوعات ناب بصورت
  تضمینی برای ارشد و دکترا.`
- BETTER_PERSIAN: `خدمات نگارش پروپوزال برای دورههای ارشد و دکتری
  ارائه میشود؛ کیفیت هر پروپوزال به تناسب موضوع و پیشینه پژوهشی
  دانشجو متفاوت است.`
- WHY: The original repeats `تضمینی` twice, chains three ezafes, and
  uses the marketing cliché `موضوعات ناب`. The rewrite is honest about
  what a service can and cannot promise.

**EX-05 — Discipline-list dump (SEO for Telegram search)**

- UNNATURAL_OR_WEAK: `پایان نامه، پروپوزال، مقاله ISI، اکسپت،
  ترجمه، ادیت نیتیو، پارافریز، کاهش سیمیلاریتی، تدوین رزومه، مصاحبه
  دکتری، ISC، JCR، Scopus، Q1، Q2 ...`
- BETTER_PERSIAN: `این مجموعه در سه حوزه اصلی فعالیت میکند: نگارش
  پروپوزال، ویرایش مقالههای انگلیسی، و مشاورهی انتشار در نشریات
  معتبر.`
- WHY: The keyword dump is not writing. The rewrite names three
  concrete service areas.

### 9.2 Emoji-cluttered Telegram post → publishable web copy

**EX-06 — Emoji bullets**

- UNNATURAL_OR_WEAK: `🔹 مرحله ۱: انتخاب موضوع ✅
  🔹 مرحله ۲: بیان مسئله ✅
  🔹 مرحله ۳: بررسی پیشینه ✅
  🔹 مرحله ۴: تعیین روش تحقیق ✅
  🔹 مرحله ۵: تحلیل داده ها ✅`
- BETTER_PERSIAN: `چهار گام اول در نگارش پایان نامه به ترتیب عبارتند
  از: انتخاب موضوع، تدوین بیان مسئله، بررسی پیشینه، و تعیین روش
  تحقیق. گام پنجم، یعنی تحلیل دادهها، پس از گردآوری دادهها اجرا
  میشود.`
- WHY: The original is a screenshot-style bullet list dressed up with
  redundant check marks. Web prose folds it into a sentence and drops
  the emoji.

**EX-07 — Emoji-titled paragraph**

- UNNATURAL_OR_WEAK: `🚨 چرا مقاله شما رد میشود؟ 🚨
  ❌ بزرگترین ضعف مقاله ای که رد میشود، مشکل علمی نیست؛ مشکل «معلوم
  نکردن هدف از نگارش» است. ✍️`
- BETTER_PERSIAN: `چرا مقاله رد میشود؟ در اغلب موارد ضعف اصلی مقالهی
  رد شده مشکل علمی نیست؛ مشکل، ابهام در بیان هدف نگارش است.`
- WHY: The siren emojis add no information; the "why" question and the
  claim stand on their own in prose.

**EX-08 — Numbered-emoji list with hashtag tail**

- UNNATURAL_OR_WEAK: `1️⃣ اصالت موضوع
  2️⃣ عنوان
  3️⃣ چکیده
  4️⃣ کلید واژه
  5️⃣ مقدمه
  #مقاله_نویسی #پایان_نامه #ISI #پژوهش #دکتری #ارشد`
- BETTER_PERSIAN: `اجزای اصلی یک مقالهی علمی پژوهشی به ترتیب عبارتند از
  اصالت موضوع، عنوان، چکیده، کلید واژهها و مقدمه.`
- WHY: The rewrite promotes the list into a single sentence and drops
  the hashtag cluster, which is Telegram search bait, not content.

**EX-09 — Emoji + `تنها` scarcity + CTA button**

## 10. Disagreements & Contested Points

- **D-1** — Software transliteration policy is genuinely inconsistent
  across channels. `analysisacademy` (S-08) keeps most software names in
  Latin (`Bibliometrix`, `Mplus`, `CiteSpace`, `VOSviewer`). `SPSS_SEM`
  (S-07) prefers Persian transliteration (`اموس`, `لیزرل`, `اسمارت پی ال
  اس`). The parent skill should pick a single house style: **Latin for
  proper software names, Persian for the technique names**
  (`آموس`/`AMOS` → `AMOS`; `تحلیل عاملی` stays Persian).
- **D-2** — `پژوهش` vs `تحقیق`. Teacher-explainer channels lean `پژوهش`;
  service channels lean `تحقیق`. Neither is wrong; the skill should
  standardize on `پژوهش` for the editorial voice.
- **D-3** — `پایان نامه` vs `پایاننامه` vs `پایان‌نامه` (ZWNJ-joined). In
  a ZWNJ-free environment (per the parent skill's ingestion rule), only
  the first two are options. The concatenated `پایاننامه` reads slightly
  more informal; the space-separated `پایان نامه` reads more editorial.
  The skill should prefer `پایان نامه` (with space).
- **D-4** — Whether `می باشد` is ever legitimate. Some senior-voice
  channels (S-04) still use it in occasional bureaucratic-tinged
  paragraphs. The parent skill should treat it as a near-universal
  rewrite target (→ `است`) but not a categorical error.
- **D-5** — Whether Fingilish loans like `پارافریز`, `سابمیت`, `اکسپت`
  are permissible in editorial prose. The convention on the more
  polished channels (S-04, S-28) is Persian equivalents (`بازنویسی`,
  `ارسال`, `پذیرش`); the coaching channels (S-14, S-19) freely mix. The
  skill should prefer Persian equivalents for the editorial register
  and allow the loans only when defining the term for a beginner
  reader.
- **D-6** — Whether religious salutations get kept when quoting from
  S-07 (SPSS_SEM). Editorial policy: strip them — they are channel
  decoration, not content.

## 11. Recommendations for the Parent Skill

1. **Ingest with register tagging.** Any Telegram-sourced passage should
   be tagged with one of the seven registers in §4 before it feeds the
   editorial model. Mixing `PROMOTIONAL_COURSE_SALES` and
   `PROFESSIONAL_EXPLAINER` in the same training slice teaches the model
   the wrong voice.
2. **Strip Telegram scaffolding first.** Emoji bullets, hashtag tails,
   contact-info footers, divider rulers, and forwarded-from headers
   should be removed by a mechanical pre-processor. What remains is the
   candidate editorial content.
3. **Rewrite bureaucratic passives categorically.** `می باشد` → `است`;
   `مورد X قرار گرفت` → `X شد`; `توسط Y … گردید` → `Y … کرد`. This is
   the single highest-leverage editorial pass on academic Persian.
4. **Cap adjacent adverbs.** Zero-or-one adverb per clause is a good
   rule. `کاملاً به راحتی و به سرعت` is a giveaway of both AI translation
   and marketing prose.
5. **Software / product names stay Latin.** `AMOS`, `LISREL`, `SmartPLS`,
   `SPSS`, `EViews`, `Turnitin`, `Zotero`, `Paperpile`, `Consensus`,
   `Elicit`, `ResearchRabbit`. Technique names (`تحلیل عاملی`, `رگرسیون`,
   `مدل معادلات ساختاری`) stay Persian.
6. **Latin acronyms stay Latin.** `ISI`, `Scopus`, `Q1`, `DOI`, `SEM`,
   `AVE`, `HTMT`, `GOF`, `ANOVA`. Do not attempt to Persianize.
7. **Do not endorse the factual claims from statistics-teacher channels
   without a citation check.** Multiple channels repeat mistakes (F-21,
   F-22, F-23). The skill should surface the passage's teaching voice,
   never its factual claims.
8. **Anti-pattern reservoir is real.** The `تضمینی`, `فرصت طلایی`,
   `تنها N ظرفیت باقی مانده`, hero-question-plus-one-word-answer patterns
   should live in the skill's `anti_patterns/promotional/` sub-catalog.
   They are diagnostic markers of Iranian thesis-service marketing prose.
9. **Question-headline templates.** `X چیست؟`, `تفاوت X و Y چیست؟`,
   `N نکته برای X`, `چگونه X را انجام دهیم؟` are legitimate editorial
   headers when the answer is substantive. `آیا هنوز X میکنید؟` and
   `چرا X میکنند اما Y نمیکنند؟` are polluted by marketing usage and
   should be avoided.
10. **Student-questionnaire register (F-48).** This is the one Telegram
    register that maps well to conversational-but-editorial Persian.
    Second person warm, apologetic-polite closing, ambient emoji. The
    skill should preserve this voice for outreach / participant-recruitment
    copy and NOT strip it to the bureaucratic register.
