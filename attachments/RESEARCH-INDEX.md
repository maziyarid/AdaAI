## Hard limits (do not pretend otherwise)

- Telegram: public `t.me/s/` previews only.
- Instagram faculty: described via Iranian academic studies, not a logged-in scrape of private profiles.
- Goodreads: public reviews/author blogs; not every Persian novelist.
- NiniSite: wrong community for thesis methods (`تحقیق` often means background-check).
- Ghostwriting / piracy channels: labelled as contamination.

## Cross-slice rules the master SKILL should not violate

1. **Split surfaces:** FAQ may echo `چطور / فرقش چیه / شما`; academic-like body stays impersonal SOV.
2. **Do not clone voiceprints:** آشوری، نجفی، صلح‌جو، سمیعی، امانی، معصومی.
3. **Do not average registers:** hedged faculty ≠ tutor hype ≠ Goodreads casual ≠ IMRaD.
4. **Locked collocations:** روایی و پایایی؛ پژوهش حاضر؛ معنادار نبود؛ بیان مسئله / پیشینه.
5. **Cut:** dummy cleft `این… است که`؛ `می‌باشد`؛ epochal openers؛ `قلب تپنده`؛ `سه سوت`؛ `راز طلایی`؛ emoji-CTA.
6. **Scientific vs literary:** پیراستگی not آراستگی (آشوری).
7. **Gauges:** کهن / دستور / گفتار / نوشتار روز (بابایی) — not a frozen 1366 list.
8. **Pair names:** student `پایان‌نامه/پروپوزال` + Irandoc `پارسا/پیشنهاده`.
9. **Tense:** proposal future; completed methods past / present-perfect.
10. **Orthography:** living academic uses نیم‌فاصله (U+200C). Teznevise site policy strips ZWNJ at publish time — house rule, not linguistics.
