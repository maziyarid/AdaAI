# Landing, product, and UX Persian

Mandatory for marketing sites, service heroes, app UI, empty states, errors, 404, CTAs, onboarding.

Qalam still writes the strings. This file is the register, not a second voice.

## Job of the page

A landing page exists so a specific Iranian reader can decide one thing. Volume is not a reason. A generic web summary is not a landing page.

Open with the job or the distinction. Never:

- در دنیای امروز
- اکوسیستم تحول آفرین
- راهکار جامع و یکپارچه
- ۷ راز
- شروع کنید (if the next screen is a form, say `بررسی اولیه پژوهش` / `نوبت حضوری بگیرید`)

## CTA

Name the action the click performs.

| Weak | Better |
|---|---|
| شروع کنید | بررسی اولیه پژوهش |
| مشاوره رایگان | ارسال طرح برای بررسی |
| دانلود جادویی | فهرست را ذخیره کنید |
| همین حالا بخرید | پرداخت هزینه ویزیت حضوری |

No fake scarcity (`ظرفیت محدود`, countdown). No guaranteed grades or surgical outcomes.

## Errors, empty, 404

- Error names the field: `نوع مقیاس متغیر وابسته را انتخاب کنید` not `مقدار نامعتبر است`.
- Empty search offers a next step.
- 404 in fa-IR, with a next step, not `404 Not Found` as the only line.

## Microcopy

Calm, specific, adult. No `کاربران عزیز`. No emoji as structure. Software names stay Latin.

Staff-facing copy is operational (`نوبت قطعی`, `پرداخت نقدی`) not developer-facing (OTP, UUID, CRM).

## Service vs landing vs guide

- Transactional intent → service owner URL. Do not write a blog that sells.
- Learning intent → guide, link to service with an educational anchor.
- Product tool → methodology, limits, crawlable fallback. No fake scores.

## Iranian market fit

Write the Persian an educated Iranian actually reads on serious product sites: معیار, readable, not منشیانه, not Telegram hustle, not Afghan Dari, not translated SaaS (`این پلتفرم به شما امکان می دهد تا از قدرت هوش مصنوعی بهره مند شوید`).

Rebuild from the job: what happens, for whom, what is not included, what happens after the click.
